Frequently asked questions
How do I get started with Tester.io? ▾
Sign up for a free account and follow the onboarding wizard — it takes under 5 minutes. You can connect your first repo or upload a test suite immediately. Our getting started guide walks you through every step.
What integrations does Tester.io support? ▾
Tester.io integrates with GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Jira, Linear, Slack, and all major CI/CD pipelines including GitHub Actions, CircleCI, and Jenkins. Native Playwright and Cypress support is built-in. See the full list on the integrations section.
Can I try Tester.io before paying? ▾
Yes — the Starter plan is completely free with no credit card required. You get up to 500 test runs per month, 2 team seats, and core platform access. Upgrade anytime as your team grows.
How does billing work for team plans? ▾
Growth and Enterprise plans are billed monthly or annually (annual saves 20%). Seats are billed per active user. You can add or remove seats at any time — adjustments are prorated automatically.
Is my data secure on Tester.io? ▾
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are SOC 2 Type II compliant and conduct annual third-party penetration tests. Read our full security overview.
Can I export my test data? ▾
Yes — you can export all test results, reports, and history in JSON or CSV format at any time. Enterprise customers also get full API access for custom data pipelines.
What's your uptime SLA? ▾
We guarantee 99.9% uptime for Growth and Enterprise plans, backed by our infrastructure on redundant multi-region cloud. You can track live status at our status page.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▾
You can cancel at any time from your account billing settings — no phone call required. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period.
