Trust & Safety

Security

Last updated: March 2026

Data Encryption

All data transmitted to and from Tester.io is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 via our infrastructure provider.

Authentication

User passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a unique salt per account. We support JWT-based session tokens with configurable expiry.

Infrastructure

Tester.io runs on Supabase-backed infrastructure with automated backups, access controls, and audit logging. Our database is never exposed to the public internet.

Access Controls

Internal access to production systems is restricted to authorised team members only, via role-based permissions and multi-factor authentication.

Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly via our contact page. We will respond within 48 hours and credit researchers who help us improve.

Compliance

We are committed to GDPR compliance for users in the EU. See our Privacy Policy for details on data processing.
