## Tuesday — How We Built It
**Image:** tue-how-we-built-it.png

People ask how a two-person team runs an entire e-commerce brand. Here's the secret: we don't do it alone.

Our tech stack does the heavy lifting:
- Next.js powers the storefront
- Supabase handles our database
- Stripe processes payments
- A Fulfillment API routes orders automatically
- Print Shop partners produce the products
- Resend delivers transactional emails

6 services. Zero manual steps. From checkout to doorstep, it's fully automated.

Build smart, not big.

**Image:** wed-order-journey.png

2. We print it — your order goes straight to our print partner
3. Quality check — every item is inspected before shipping
4. Shipped to you — tracking updates sent automatically
5. Unbox happiness — your custom pet gear arrives!

The entire process takes 2–3 business days. No warehouses. No inventory risk. Just on-demand magic.
