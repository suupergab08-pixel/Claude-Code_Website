What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us recognise you, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our platform.

Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — Required for the platform to function (authentication, session management). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand how users interact with our pages so we can improve them. Anonymised and aggregated.
  • Preference cookies — Remember your settings such as theme and language preferences.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services (such as analytics providers) that set their own cookies. These are governed by the respective third party's privacy policy.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the platform from functioning correctly.
